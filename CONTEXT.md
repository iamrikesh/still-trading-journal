# still. trading journal

A personal journal for noticing reactions during trading and reflecting on them afterward. Its language distinguishes what was captured at the time from later interpretation.

## Language

**Trading session**:
A trading period explicitly started and ended by the user, with at most one active at a time; it can span midnight and contain multiple moments. A moment can also exist outside a trading session.
_Avoid_: Trading day, trade, recording session

**Moment**:
A timestamped capture of the user's reaction or thought, with or without an emotion label, that may belong to a trading session. Capturing a moment does not require a note or recording.
_Avoid_: Trade, reflection

**Original capture**:
The account recorded at the time of a moment; once finalised, it is preserved rather than silently rewritten by later interpretation.
_Avoid_: Reflection, current opinion

**Note draft**:
An unfinished typed account whose text is saved automatically for recovery until the user chooses Done to finalise it, even after its trading session ends. Finalisation has its own time, separate from the original capture.
_Avoid_: Final note, reflection

**Reflection**:
A separately dated later interpretation of an individual moment or a whole trading session that leaves the original capture intact. A finished reflection is preserved; a changed view is recorded as a new dated follow-up.
_Avoid_: Correction of the original, replacement note

**Personal reminder**:
The user's support text, audio or image shown in response to an emotion or urge. Playing reminder audio is a separate choice from recording a new journal voice clip.
_Avoid_: Journal recording, reflection

**Archived session**:
A trading session hidden from the normal session list but retained with its contents for viewing and restoration.
_Avoid_: Deleted session
